What this is

A 1995 GBC resolution called for a consolidated Law Book. The last edition anyone can read is from 2018. This draft takes that edition as its starting point. Published resolutions from 2019 to 2026 are placed under the book's chapters, each with a reference to its source document. The result is 422 pages with search and source links.

The mechanical work is done. The legal and institutional review is not.
